# Internal FAQ

## Who owns platform incidents?
Platform Engineering owns infrastructure, Kubernetes, core APIs, and database availability incidents. Security owns credential exposure, suspicious access, and confirmed security events.

## Where should employees ask policy questions?
Employees should consult the HR handbook first and escalate ambiguous cases to HR Operations.

## Can an AI assistant create incidents automatically?
No. AI-generated incident proposals require a human to review the title, description, priority, and owning team before the incident is created.
