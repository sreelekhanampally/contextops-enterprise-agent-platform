# Human Resources Policy Handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave should be requested through the HR portal and, except for emergencies, approved by the employee's manager before travel is booked. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees may take up to 12 paid sick-leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required when an absence exceeds two consecutive working days.

## Remote work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week when the arrangement does not conflict with customer, security, or operational requirements. Managers may temporarily suspend remote-work privileges during major incidents or customer launches.
